Organic Sourcing & Purity

Are your spices 100% certified USDA Organic?

Yes! All our spices, rice, grains, and pulses are sourced from accredited organic farm cooperatives holding USDA Organic, EU Organic, and India Organic certifications. We never use synthetic pesticides or chemical fertilizers.

What does cold stone-milled mean?

Commercial high-speed steel hammer mills generate excessive heat (above 70°C) during grinding, which evaporates delicate aromatic oils and degrades medicinal curcumin. Our heritage granite mills rotate slowly at low temperatures, keeping 100% of the active volatile oils intact.

Shipping & Freshness Guarantee

All orders of $50 or more automatically qualify for Free Doorstep Shipping with expedited courier dispatch (2-4 business days). Orders under $50 ship at a low flat rate of $4.99.
